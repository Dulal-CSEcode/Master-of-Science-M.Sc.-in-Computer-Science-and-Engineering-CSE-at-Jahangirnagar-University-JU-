## 👥 Group Learning Activity (Jigsaw Learning)

The class was divided into **8 groups**, with **6 students in each group**.

### Step 1: Topic Assignment
Sir selected **six different topics** from Distributed Systems. Within each group, every member was assigned **one topic**.

Students were given **15 minutes** to study and understand their assigned topic individually.

---

### Step 2: Expert Group Discussion

After individual study, students working on the **same topic from all 8 groups** formed an **Expert Group**.

- Each Expert Group consisted of students having the same topic.
- They discussed their topic in detail.
- Shared knowledge, clarified concepts, and solved doubts together.
- Discussion time: **10 minutes**

This allowed every student to become an **expert** on their assigned topic.

---

### Step 3: Return to Original Groups

After the Expert Group discussion, all students returned to their **original groups**.

Each student then explained their expert topic to the other group members.

As a result, every group member learned **all six topics** through peer teaching.

Each student could give a **2–3 minute mini-presentation** on their assigned topic within the group.

---

### 🎤 Final Presentation

At the end of the activity:

- Sir selected **one student from each of the 8 groups**.
- The selected student **did not present their own assigned topic**.
- Instead, they presented **another group member's topic**.
- This evaluated how effectively knowledge had been shared within the group.

Finally, **the best-performing group** was selected based on overall understanding and presentation.
